About

Engineer on the plant floor, developer at the keyboard.

I'm an Electrical & Electronics Engineer specialized in PLC programming, industrial automation and control systems. I currently work as an Automation Engineer at ITC Invest Trading & Consulting AG in Ankara, where I'm responsible for the automation infrastructure of a recycling and waste-processing facility — waste-to-energy production, water treatment and incineration processes.

My background covers the full spectrum: designing press machines up to 1500 tons in EPLAN and programming them on Siemens S7 platforms at Dirinler Makina, managing high-voltage systems and transformer substations as an Engineering Lieutenant in the Turkish Air Force, and building production web applications that our teams use every day — a live fleet route-optimization platform and a facility inspection app.

What makes my profile a bit unusual is the combination: I'm equally comfortable writing ladder logic for a sterilization machine, wiring a KEPServerEX→SQL data pipeline, and shipping a React + Node.js application with real-time GPS tracking. I like owning problems end to end — from the sensor to the dashboard.

Saha Kontrol — Facility Inspection PWA

A tablet application that replaced manual Excel inspection forms across multiple industrial facilities covering thousands of equipment items. Field technicians score equipment, take photos and sync — even with no connection.

  • Photo capture automatically filed by equipment KKS code; manager dashboard with KPIs, area×criteria matrix and a prioritized action list.
